# ROADSTER OVERDRIVE

**Drive the impossible.** A free browser 3D arcade highway runner by Santiago & Pablo:
dodge sunset traffic, hover on cold-gas thrusters, land barrel rolls and front flips,
then unleash Rocket Mode.

This repository only contains the compiled production build served by GitHub Pages.

- Controls: arrows/A-D lanes, Space jump (hold to fly), S/↓ slam, Shift boost, Q/E rolls, F flip, X Rocket Mode, Esc pause. Touch and gamepad supported.
- Third-party content: traffic & debris models from Kenney's Car Kit (CC0, kenney.nl); fonts Saira & Saira Stencil One (SIL OFL 1.1); three.js (MIT). License texts are in `licenses/`.
- Music and sound effects are synthesized in the browser; vehicles and world are procedural and original.

Unofficial fan-inspired driving game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla or SpaceX.
